# Site stats

A small, public view of how devcol.net is doing: page views, game plays, and where visitors come from. The numbers come from DevCol's own analytics, which uses no cookies, does not store IP addresses, and skips browsers with Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control turned on. Details are in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).

Totals are updated once a day. Visitors are counted per day, so one person visiting on three different days counts three times.
