# About DevCol

DevCol ("development collaboration") is an indie game built in the open, using Unreal Engine. The project is developed by a solo/small team, and the goal is simple: build the game where the people who'll eventually play it can watch, react, and influence it along the way — instead of waiting until launch to hear from anyone.

## What "in the open" means here

- **Devlog** — regular, honest progress notes: what got built, what broke, what changed direction and why. Not just polished trailers.
- **Wiki** — living documentation of the game's systems and lore as they're designed, not a marketing sheet written after the fact.
- **Community first** — feedback from Discord, YouTube comments, and social channels is treated as real input into design decisions, not just engagement.

## Why this site exists

This site serves three purposes:

1. A **devlog** archive of the game's development.
2. A **wiki** for game systems, lore, and FAQ as they're built out.
3. A lightweight **portfolio** of the work behind DevCol.
