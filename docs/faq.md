# FAQ

## About DevCol

### What is DevCol?

DevCol is short for "development collaboration." It's an indie game project built in Unreal Engine by one developer, with progress shared in the [DevLog](/blog/) as it happens. This site is where I keep the record of it: what I build, what breaks, and what I learn from fixing it. There's more on the [About](/about) page.

### Who makes it?

One person makes the games and runs the site. Some of the projects and parts of this site were made with Claude's help, and the pages say so where it applies. The [Tools (AI-Assisted)](/projects/#tools) projects are marked that way, and the [devcol.net project page](/projects/devcol-net) explains how this site was built.

### What are you working on now?

PSEUDO, a stylish action RPG in Unreal Engine 5 where a 2d6 dice roll decides the result of your skills, with a multiplayer arena mode. The DevLog so far covers the repository setup and the first framework bug. You can find everything on the [PSEUDO](/blog/pseudo) page. Before it, I built Tumble Teams, a multiplayer warm-up project, and its notes are on the [Tumble Teams](/blog/tumble-teams) page.

### When is it releasing?

There's no release date yet. The game is still early. Progress updates go in the [DevLog](/blog/) and on [Discord](https://discord.gg/7YTgpP8CCt).

## The games

### What can I play right now?

Two Unity games are released on Google Play: [Press Plane](/projects/press-plane) and [Rock Paper Scissors - Advance](/projects/rock-paper-scissors-advance). Both also run in the browser on their project pages, and on itch.io.

### Should I play on my phone or in the browser?

On a phone, use the Android app. The browser versions are meant for trying a game from a computer, and the Rock Paper Scissors web build doesn't run well in mobile browsers.

### Is the code public?

Some of it. Press Plane, Rock Paper Scissors - Advance, NewsBriefing, and this site have repositories on GitHub under [devcol-main](https://github.com/devcol-main), and each project page links to its own. Check a repository's license before reusing its code.

## The site

### What are the DevLog, Projects, and Study sections?

The [DevLog](/blog/) has progress notes and debugging write-ups from PSEUDO and Tumble Teams, including the mistakes. [Projects](/projects/) collects the games, prototypes, and small tools I've finished. [Study](/study/) holds shorter notes on Unreal Engine, C++, and tools that I wanted to remember.

### Does the site collect data or show ads?

You don't need an account, and the site doesn't ask for personal information to read it. Like most websites, it may use cookies through third-party services such as Google AdSense. The [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) has the details, including how to opt out of personalized ads.

### Is there a Korean version?

Not yet. The site is in English, and a Korean version is planned for later.
