# Bukang Run

A shark is stuck in a narrow harbor canal. Swim it out to the open sea: switch lanes, jump over low nets, dive under high ones, and grab ice cream on the way. The game text is in Korean, but the controls below are all you need.

<GameFrame slug="bukang-run" title="Bukang Run, a three-lane shark swimming runner" hint="Click the game once, then the arrow keys work too. The first load is about 9 MB." />

## Controls

- **Left / Right, A / D, or swipe sideways**: change lanes.
- **Up, W, Space, or swipe up**: jump. Clears low nets, buoys and the water pump.
- **Down, S, or swipe down and hold**: dive. Passes under high nets, camera flashes and oil slicks. Diving uses oxygen, so you can't stay down forever.
- **Shift, or hold the round 가속 (boost) button**: swim 1.4 times faster and earn distance points twice as fast.
- **Tap or Space**: start, and start again after a game over.

## Tips

- High nets and oil slicks cover all three lanes. The only way past is to dive, so keep some oxygen in reserve.
- Boost when the lane ahead is clear and let go before a section with several obstacles in a row.
- The route changes every few hundred meters: the canal, the barrier net zone, Busan Port and finally the open sea. Each one adds new obstacles.
