# Rock Paper Scissors - Advance

The hand game everyone knows, played against a timer. Pick one of three modes and keep winning before the clock runs out.

<GameFrame src="https://itch.io/embed-upload/15989559?color=333333" href="https://devcol.itch.io/rock-paper-scissors-advance" ratio="2 / 3" title="Rock Paper Scissors - Advance, a reflex game with three modes" hint="Made in Unity and hosted on itch.io. Click to load. Best on a desktop browser." />

## Modes

- **Regular**: the classic game. You and the AI pick at the same moment on a count of three. If time runs out before you choose, you lose.
- **Response**: the AI shows its hand first, and you pick the hand that beats it before time is up. The timer gets shorter as your score goes up.
- **Reverse**: the win rules are flipped. Rock beats Paper, Paper beats Scissors, and Scissors beats Rock. Your first instinct is the wrong answer.

## Controls

- **Click** a hand, or press **1** for Rock, **2** for Paper, **3** for Scissors.
- The web build doesn't run well in mobile browsers. On a phone, use the [Android app on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.devcol.rockpaperscissorsadvance).

How the modes are judged and how the timer speeds up is on the [Rock Paper Scissors - Advance project page](/projects/rock-paper-scissors-advance).
