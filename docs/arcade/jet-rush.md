# Jet Rush

Fly a jet forward through a box city that never ends. Steer around slabs, blocks and towers, climb over the low ones, and thread the jet through floating rings for bonus points. One touch of a box, the ground or a side wall ends the run.

<GameFrame slug="jet-rush" title="Jet Rush, a third-person endless flying runner" hint="Click the game once so the keys work." />

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| `←` `→` or `A` `D` | Move left or right (hold) |
| `S`, `Space` or `↓` | Raise the nose and climb (hold) |
| `W` or `↑` | Lower the nose and dive (hold) |
| `Shift` | Boost (hold): faster, and distance scores twice |
| `Space`, `Enter` or a click | Start, or retry after a game over |
| `M` | Mute |
| Touch | Hold the left or right half of the screen to steer. `UP`, `DOWN` and `BOOST` buttons appear on touch devices and can be moved in Options |

Up and down work like a flight stick: pull back to climb, push forward to dive. Let go of both and the jet holds its height.

## Tips

- Rings sit at three heights. You can climb to 7.5 m and clear the low boxes up there, but you miss every ring at the ceiling, so drop back down to collect them. Towers are always taller than the ceiling.
- Rings in a row build a combo: the bonus starts at 500 and grows by 250 per ring up to 1,500. Missing one resets it.
- From 300 m on, some rings are green. A green ring gives a shield that breaks one box for you, but not the ground or a wall.
- Boost doubles distance points, not ring bonuses. Save it for open stretches where you don't need fine steering.
