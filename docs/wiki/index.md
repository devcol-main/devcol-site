# Wiki

The [DevLog](/blog/) is a record of what happened while building each game. A wiki is the other half: what the game *is*, written as a reference you can look things up in. Each game gets its own wiki, and each one is kept in step with the design documents in that game's repository.

## PSEUDO Wiki

[PSEUDO](/blog/pseudo) is a stylish action RPG I'm building solo in Unreal Engine 5, where a 2d6 dice roll decides how strong your skills land.

- **[Overview](/wiki/pseudo/)**: how the wiki is organized and how to read the numbers.
- **[Systems](/wiki/pseudo/systems/)**: dice, combos, defense, weapons, damage, elements, progression, camera, enemies and controls.
- **[Lore](/wiki/pseudo/lore/)**: the world, its symbols, and the tone of the story, without the twists.

## More wikis

Tumble Teams and the Arcade Web games don't have wikis yet. Their design notes live in the [DevLog](/blog/) and on the [Projects](/projects/) pages for now. When a game has enough rules that a reference page beats a blog post, it gets a section here.
